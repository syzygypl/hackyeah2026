# Legal check (PL/EU): Airlock and Rój pitch claims

By AI Mateusza, 2026-10-03 ~12:50, for the GS and open AI pitches. **Not legal advice**: a fast desk check of public sources so we don't over-promise on stage. Inputs: `keynote.md`, `brand.md`, the deck (artifact SRwnPm...), `spikes/ai-control-layer/README.md` + `policy.json`, `swarm-concept.md`, `roj-x-acl.md`, `local-models.md`, `general-rules.txt`, `docs/tasks/*`.

**Headline:** compliance is a good selling point if we say "supports" and "helps you evidence", never "makes you compliant". Two facts in our own repo contradict our own wording: `policy.json` allowlists a cloud model (`claude-sonnet-5-5`), and Rój's arbiter is an optional cloud API. So "Nothing leaves" is not true as written.

## 2. Pitch-safe sentences (PL / EN)

1. **EN:** "Every decision lands in a tamper-evident log, the kind of record AI Act Article 12 and DORA expect you to keep." **PL:** "Każda decyzja trafia do dziennika, w którym widać każdą manipulację. To taki zapis, jakiego wymagają art. 12 AI Act i DORA."
2. **EN:** "Risky actions wait for a person. That supports the human oversight Article 14 of the AI Act asks for." **PL:** "Ryzykowne działania czekają na człowieka. To wspiera nadzór człowieka, o którym mówi art. 14 AI Act."
3. **EN:** "The models run on your hardware, so prompts don't go to a model provider and no data transfer outside the EU is needed." **PL:** "Modele działają na twoim sprzęcie. Prompty nie trafiają do dostawcy modelu, a dane nie muszą opuszczać UE."
4. **EN:** "PESEL, IBAN and card numbers are masked before the model sees them: privacy by design in the GDPR Article 25 sense, built into the gateway." **PL:** "PESEL, IBAN i numery kart są maskowane, zanim zobaczy je model. To privacy by design w rozumieniu art. 25 RODO, wbudowane w bramkę."
5. **EN (Rój):** "Rój quotes the letter word for word, or says 'I don't know'. You check, you decide." **PL:** "Rój cytuje pismo słowo w słowo albo mówi 'nie wiem, sprawdź'. Ty sprawdzasz, ty decydujesz."

Always add when asked: "It supports compliance; it doesn't certify it. It's a hackathon prototype."
