## Observation for the calibration (engine side, not changed here)

On some simulated cases the engine auto-expands the grid to cover the evidence (e.g. kasprowy case-0003: 72 km² grid) while the segment list stays the one of the region scenario, so far cells join existing segments and segments become very large ("top 3 segments hold 99% in 67% of the area"). Segment-level hit rates are then too easy; please also report cell-level metrics (% of area searched in POA order until the true cell).
